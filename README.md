# Havoc City

A polished 3D open-world action playground built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WebGL, and Three.js.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then visit <http://localhost:4173>.

## Controls

- **WASD** — camera-relative movement and strafing / drive
- **Mouse** — free-look third-person camera
- **Shift** — sprint / boost
- **E** — talk to nearby NPCs, enter vehicles, or exit a vehicle
- **Space** — jump on foot
- **F or left click** — attack and destroy nearby scenery
- **H** — toggle collision-box debugging

Havoc City includes a free-look third-person camera with standard camera-relative movement and strafing; fixed-timestep physics with gravity, jumping, collision boxes and impact testing; a cinematic tropical city with a sunset sky, palms, waterfront, neon storefronts, and illuminated streets; twelve usable vehicles across cars, bikes, a skateboard, planes, and boats; textured, facially animated characters; animated wheels and propellers; exhaust smoke, boat wakes, animated water; six interactive NPCs; physics-driven destruction debris; a wanted-level system; a mission; a live minimap; dynamic lighting and shadows; and responsive UI.
